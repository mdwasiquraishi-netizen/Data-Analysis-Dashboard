# Personal-Finance-Data-Analysis (Interactive Dashboard using MS Excel)
## Project Objective
The Main Objective of this Project is to track total income and Expenses. Support Better Budgeting and financial planning decisions.
## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/mdwasiquraishi-netizen/Data-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/main/FInance%20corporation%20dataset.xlsx">Dataset</a>
## Questions for the Dashboard
-What percentage of the budget has been utilized?
-Which expense categories are over budget?
-Which categories are under budget?
-Where is the biggest difference between budget and actual spending?
-Which income category generates the highest income?
-How much income comes from salary?
-How much income is generated from freelancing, interest, and bonuses?
-Which month has the highest income
-Which category has the highest expenses?
-How much is being spent on dining, electricity, fuel, grocery, healthcare, internet, rent, and shopping?
-Which month has the highest expenses?
-Are expenses increasing or decreasing over time?
-Which bank has the highest number of transactions?
-Which payment method is used for the highest amount of expenses?
-How much is spent through UPI, Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, Cash, and Bank Transfer?
-Which savings goal requires the highest amount?
-How much money is allocated toward different financial goals?
-Is the current saving rate sufficient to achieve future financial goals?

## Project Insights
1)The dashboard shows ₹3,836K total income and ₹3,452K total expenses, resulting in ₹384K net savings.
2)The overall saving rate is 10%, indicating an opportunity to improve savings through better expense management.
3)Budget utilization is 93%, meaning most of the allocated budget has already been utilized.
4)Freelancing is the highest income source at ₹1,167K, followed by Salary at ₹978K.
5)UPI has the highest expense value at approximately ₹621K among all payment methods.
6)Dining, Electricity, Healthcare, and Shopping are among the major expense categories requiring regular monitoring.
7)Vacation has the highest savings goal at ₹2,051K, followed by Car at ₹1,968K.
8)Axis Bank has the highest number of transactions with 119 transactions among the displayed banks.
9)The monthly income vs. expense analysis shows significant fluctuations throughout FY 2026, highlighting the importance of monthly cash-flow tracking.
